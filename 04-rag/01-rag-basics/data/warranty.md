# NimbusKart Warranty

All NimbusKart-brand electronics come with a 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects.

## Extended warranty
Customers can buy NimbusCare extended warranty for 2 extra years at 10 percent of the product price. NimbusCare must be bought within 15 days of delivery.

## What is not covered
Physical damage, water damage and damage from unauthorised repairs are not covered by the warranty.

## Claiming warranty
Raise a claim from the Orders page. A technician visit is scheduled within 3 business days for large appliances; small items are picked up and repaired within 10 business days.
