# NimbusKart Refund Policy

NimbusKart offers a 30-day refund window for most products. The 30 days start from the delivery date, not the order date.

## Eligible items
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Electronics must include all accessories and the original box.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards, digital downloads, perishable groceries and personalised items cannot be refunded.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 7 business days. UPI refunds are usually completed within 48 hours. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded as NimbusKart wallet credit.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Damaged-on-arrival items get a full refund including shipping charges.
