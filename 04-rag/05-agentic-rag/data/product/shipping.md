# NimbusKart Shipping Guide

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and 5 to 8 business days elsewhere.

## Express shipping
Express shipping delivers within 24 hours in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Hyderabad. It costs 99 rupees per order and is free for NimbusKart Plus members.

## Free shipping
Orders above 499 rupees get free standard shipping.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by SMS and email once it is dispatched. Tracking updates every 4 hours.

## International shipping
NimbusKart does not ship outside India at the moment.
