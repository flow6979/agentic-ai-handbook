# NimbusKart Plus Membership

NimbusKart Plus costs 999 rupees per year or 129 rupees per month.

## Benefits
Plus members get free express shipping, early access to sales, and 5 percent cashback as wallet credit on every order.

## Cancellation
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Yearly plans can be cancelled within 14 days for a full refund; after that, no refund is given for the remaining months.
