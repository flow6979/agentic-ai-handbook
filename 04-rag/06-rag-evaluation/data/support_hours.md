# Customer Support

NimbusKart customer support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM IST, all seven days of the week.

Customers can reach support through in-app chat, email at help@nimbuskart.example, or phone at 1800-000-1234. Plus members get a priority phone line with an average wait time under 2 minutes.

Escalations are handled by a senior agent within 24 hours.
